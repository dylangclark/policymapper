How to read this explorer

Connections are associations supplied in the workbook. Counts indicate how often a code was assigned, not effectiveness, severity, prevalence, or research priority. Outcomes link separately to failures and enablers; there are no assumed failure–enabler links.

By default selection highlights direct neighbours. “Include connections through outcomes” reveals the other codes attached to the same outcomes when a failure or enabler is selected. These are shared associations, not demonstrated causal links. Theme filters apply to both views. Codes with no outcomes in the selected theme remain visible in the charts with zero counts.

Repeated codes and duplicate outcome–code links count once. The source workbook is authoritative; the generator does not apply changes from earlier discussions. Outcome positions follow the selected screening dimension, highest score first; ties retain workbook order. Colours and numeric labels show the selected dimension. Combined score adds economic risk, governance risk, salience and urgency with equal weights (4–12); all four ratings must be present. It is a screening sum, not a policy-effectiveness measure. Unknown, blank, N/A and unmatched scores are neutral and placed last. The optional Theme / original order restores the unranked layout. Ratings are provisional; wellbeing and natural environment remain shelved. Failure and enabler positions are for readability.

Policy bubbles are candidate associations: a policy must link to the outcome, the policy must address/support or potentially worsen/constrain the code, and the outcome–code edge must already exist. This join is not independent validation of a specific three-way causal mechanism or proof of effectiveness. No edge is added merely to place a policy. Potential adverse roles use dashed borders; a policy can have both supportive and adverse roles. Repeated policy names represent associations, not independent interventions.

Policy names stay attached to the edge curves while nodes move, the map pans/zooms or outcomes are reordered. The default avoids label overlap; the status reports how many fit. Select a node, filter a policy, zoom, allow overlap or use the policy association list to access remaining names. Full names and assessment notes are available on click.
