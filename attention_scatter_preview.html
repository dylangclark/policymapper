Each marker is one adaptation outcome. Horizontal position is its importance score; vertical position is its attention score. Hover or focus on a marker for the outcome and its contract and B.C. grant counts. Click a point to pin its details.

Source: Export outcome to expenditures_strong.csv. Grant counts refer to B.C. grants. A score of zero is plotted as provided, not interpreted as proof that no relevant activity exists. Points with identical scores are separated slightly on screen only to make individual outcomes selectable; scores are not changed. Counts are assigned to outcomes and should not be summed across outcomes without accounting for records matched to more than one outcome. These scores and counts are independent of the research treemap's filters.
